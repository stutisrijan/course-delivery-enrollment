### Prompt 1 

I need to build a Course Delivery & Enrollment web application for a company assignment with approximately 12 hours of development time. The application requires authentication with instructor and learner roles, courses, lessons, publishing/archive states, learner enrollment and progress tracking, server-side search/filtering/pagination, bulk enrollment with CSV export, an instructor dashboard, immutable activity logs, and inactivity alerts.

Suggest a simple full-stack technology stack that is fast to develop, easy to deploy on free tiers,  and appropriate for relational data. Prioritize technologies that are stable and easy to understand rather than introducing unnecessary complexity.

### What I got

The recommended stack was React + Vite for the frontend, Node.js + Express for the backend, PostgreSQL with Prisma for the database layer, JWT and bcrypt for authentication, and Vercel/Render/Supabase for deployment.

### What I corrected

No correction at this stage. The proposed stack was selected because it fits the assignment's relational requirements and limited development time
